01 About

Production AI at Scale

I'm a Senior Engineer in Production Engineering at Clear Street, building AI-powered operational intelligence for a multi-asset clearing house — incident investigation, alerting, anomaly detection, and conversational ops tooling used across 15+ engineering teams and 50+ global stakeholders. I joined as an embedded vendor engineer via Wissen in 2024 and converted to full-time in 2025 on the back of delivery.

Before that: Morgan Stanley (VP, Payments, Treasury & Cash Management), Barclays, and Cognizant. The thread through all of it — the people who need to act on a system's output are rarely the people who can read its internals, so the output has to work for both.

I design and teach the AI automation curriculum for IIT Patna's Generative AI Programme, and co-founded TransmuteLabs, an AI consulting practice, with my wife Sruthi (Lead Data Scientist at EY).
